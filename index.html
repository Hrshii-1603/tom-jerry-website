Their legendary story

It All Started With A Mouse!

Every legend has a beginning, and theirs started back in 1940.

Originally introduced in the animated short Puss Gets the Boot, Tom and Jerry quickly became one of the most loved cartoon duos in the world.

Created by William Hanna and Joseph Barbera, their adventures proved something magical: you don't need endless dialogue to make people laugh.

From their first chase to countless unforgettable moments, their story became a timeless part of childhood.

86years of laughter, chaos and unforgettable memories!
